# AI Stalemate proposal: review and adoption

Branch: `ai-stalemate-library-proposal`. Base: `field-tools-launch` at `5e0241c`. Deployment: `rebel-ai-stalemate-proposal` only. Review desk: https://rebel-ai-stalemate-proposal-production.up.railway.app/field-tools/proposal

## What the buyer can now do

Choose their role-specific page, inspect one inventory and the visual kit, open their start page, prepare privately, and work the ongoing conversation. They can invite their counterpart into a worthwhile future, propose or record the next move, and adapt updates for leadership and the team. A bounded Bet is optional. If a test is appropriate, its effort and quality are separate records, and deployment or expansion needs its own permission. Returning after the action makes learning and changing working conditions part of the experience.

The Guides preserve the existing twelve teaching chapters, add five companion pages, and link to matching Lab steps and live resources. Each PDF has eighteen pages. The five companions address vision, shared discussion, the governing Bet, communication/quality/return, and Identity/Relationship/Vision/Culture. The library has sixteen tool entries and ten study/framework entries, with distinct PM and engineering questions.

## Review through Nic's lens

- A useful next move comes before an experiment or a pile of documents. The worked case ends at preparation, without invented gains.
- Communication names shared context and a future worth shaping; disagreement can remain explicit. PM and lead-developer paths retain their distinct perspective.
- Private motive-reading and personal reflection are not saved or exported. Shared drafts are editable and remain on the user's device.
- Evidence counts all six effort stages for both methods. Quality has expected/observed results, references, verifiers, and NOT CHECKED/PASSED/FAILED states.
- Frameworks serve the situation: an appetite is an investment limit, RAPID separates decision rights, and a short update ends with an actionable request.
- Resources supply a reason, relevant section where available, and a question to bring back. Vendor interviews and dated studies are labeled. No tool is presented as proof of enterprise safety.
- Leadership formation is a short return loop and a prompt to improve the conditions around others. Wider Rebel Leaders links remain optional.

Self-review corrections: body copy now uses the UI font; each role has its own actual Lab image; starter-source cards clear filters and jump after rendering; example communication has concrete support requests rather than generic request placeholders; shared vision is available during Conversation, before any Bet; freeform defect notes no longer duplicate the structured checks.

## Selective adoption groups

1. Product page: `AIProductPage`, `AIKitPreview`, `ai-product`, their CSS/assets, the landing dispatch, and the `/buy` redirect. Retire the old PM landing component.
2. Lab and output: communication and quality modules/panels, `FieldToolLab`, readiness/path content and samples, export builders, privacy exclusion, and tests. Existing IDs, local draft version, and step ordering remain compatible.
3. Guide: companion data, gated content wiring, `AIGuideVisual`, `ChapterGuide`, PDF build script and the two PDFs. Preserve the existing scroll highlighting. Changing the page count also requires the inventory and review links to agree.
4. Library and walkthrough: resource data, gated pages/routes, the start-page links, and the maintenance instructions. Their content must remain gated on the live service.

These groups have dependencies; accepting individual files without their imports, routes, or gated data may break the build. Use a deliberate follow-up commit for selective adoption rather than merging only arbitrary files.

## Verification and limits

The focused workflow, privacy, readiness, follow-through, leadership, commerce, and proposal tests pass (75 tests). TypeScript and the production build pass. Both PDFs were rendered and inspected, including the complete page overview and full-size companion pages. Browser checks cover the unified PM product/checklist, actual PDF download, role-specific Lab wording, stakeholder draft generation and brief rendering, failed quality entry, tool filtering, and the walkthrough's final record.

The review environment takes no payment. It does not validate live receipts, email sign-in, payment, refunds, or order fulfillment; those require configured commerce and an actual end-to-end test. The worked walkthrough is interactive; a recorded-video URL is not configured. CSS supports narrower layouts, but an actual phone/device pass is still needed. Resource maintenance is manual. The other four products' content and PDFs are unchanged by this proposal.
