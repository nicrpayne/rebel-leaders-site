# AI Stalemate resource review

The library is a curated manual resource list. It is not an automatically maintained feed, a claim of enterprise safety, or a promise of perpetual updates. Sources were reviewed on 12 October 2026. Tool cards link to official documentation; study cards distinguish research, practitioner experience, vendor interviews, books, and frameworks.

Review before each release and schedule an editorial review monthly if the product is launched. Check destination URLs, current names, relevant sections, plan availability, limitations, and whether the reader question still helps. Update the review date only after that review. Preserve historical dates on studies and pair the METR 2025 study with the 2026 update. Never present a dated result as a universal claim about current tools.

The shared resource data is `server/field-tool-content/ai-resources.ts`. The gated resource UI is `client/src/pages/AIResources.tsx`. Sources are supplied only through authorized FieldToolGate content. Public product copy describes the inventory without exposing the paid library.

The product's recorded-video URL is configured through `FIELD_TOOL_AI_STALEMATE_WALKTHROUGH_URL`. Until supplied, the worked walkthrough remains a real interactive example. Do not advertise an existing recorded video without a playable URL.
