# AI STALEMATE — V1 PRODUCT CONTRACT

**Brand:** Rebel Leaders / Field Tool 01  
**Price:** $47 one-time  
**Goal:** put a real paid offer in front of Product and Engineering leaders quickly enough to learn whether the problem, promise, and delivery are valuable.

## Customer

A Product Manager, Product Owner, Product Lead, Engineering Lead, Tech Lead, or Engineering Manager stuck in a recurring disagreement about whether and how AI should be used in software/product work.

Two valid starting sentences:

- **Product / Leadership:** “My lead dev won’t try AI.”
- **Engineering / Tech Leadership:** “My PM won’t shut up about AI.”

There is no hero/villain split. Both are leaders. Both may be protecting something legitimate.

## Buying moment

Use this when:

- the same AI conversation has happened more than once;
- Product sees opportunity while Engineering sees transferred cost or risk;
- Engineering objections are being experienced as blanket resistance;
- leadership wants an “AI answer” but the team does not have credible evidence;
- AI use is mandated but workflow, boundaries, evidence, capacity, or ownership are unclear;
- a previous attempt generated opinions but not a decision;
- the team is arguing about “AI” broadly instead of a specific use in a specific workflow.

Not for general AI education, prompt libraries, tool comparisons, coding tutorials, or universal productivity claims.

## Promise

### Short
**Stop having the same AI argument. Design one test you could both believe.**

### Full
In one short preparation and one 30-minute working session, turn an AI stalemate into a useful next decision: a bounded trial, a named prerequisite, a justified stop, or a clear decision owner.

We do **not** promise adoption or productivity. We promise a better decision process.

## What the buyer gets

1. **Product / Leadership Field Guide** — observation vs interpretation, desired improvement, Engineering’s cost, conversation prep.
2. **Engineering / Tech Leadership Field Guide** — transferred cost, observed failure vs prediction, boundaries, what would change the view.
3. **Shared Workbook** — Trial Card → Evidence Record → Continue / Revise / Stop / Pause → Decision Brief.
4. **AI Stalemate Lab** — no-account browser instrument guiding the same flow.
5. **7–10 minute walkthrough** — one fictional case from argument to decision.
6. **Working Edition updates** — improvements during V1 working-edition period.

## Three useful outputs

### Trial Card
Defines the work outcome, exact task, exclusions, tool/version, approved inputs, approval owner, acceptance checks, accountable engineering owner, comparator, protected time, displaced work, success bar, stop rule, owners, and review date.

### Evidence Record
Counts setup/context, drafting, author verification/fixes, reviewer checking/rework, follow-up fixes, acceptance checks, defects, elapsed time, tool cost, learning effects, and later defects.

### Decision Brief
Records problem, tested task, evidence, limits of evidence, Continue/Revise/Stop/Pause, disagreement, next action, accountable owner, date, and a one-sentence update to leadership.

## User journey

### Private prep — 5–10 minutes
- What actually happened?
- What am I interpreting?
- What am I protecting?
- What have I observed?
- What is still prediction?
- What would change my mind?
- What do I still need to ask?

### Shared session — 30 minutes
**00–05:** Clarify the request and work outcome.  
**05–12:** Make Product’s opportunity and Engineering’s cost visible.  
**12–22:** Use prior evidence or define one bounded trial if useful uncertainty remains.  
**22–30:** Complete the Trial Card or name the missing prerequisite / decision owner.

### Review — 15 minutes after the trial
Compare real evidence to the agreed bar. Choose:
**CONTINUE / REVISE / STOP / PAUSE.**

## Product principles

1. No villain.
2. Observation before motive.
3. The proposal is allowed to change.
4. A failed AI use case can be a successful leadership outcome.
5. STOP must be a legitimate result.
6. Count the full cost.
7. Drafting speed is not team productivity if checking cost moved elsewhere.
8. This tool does not create decision rights.
9. Small trials create local evidence, not universal productivity claims.
10. Private preparation stays private; only agreed facts become shared output.

## Why not “just ask AI”?

A general AI assistant can give advice. This product is a **designed protocol for two humans carrying different consequences**.

Its value is the sequence, symmetry, decision-rights guardrails, full-cost evidence, shared artifacts, stop rule, and decision record.

**AI can help you think. This helps two leaders decide.**
